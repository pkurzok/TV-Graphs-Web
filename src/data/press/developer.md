Peter Kurzok is an independent developer from Germany building native apps for Apple platforms — previous releases include PlayTales, a companion for audiobook fans, and Photo Memo+. TV Graphs is written entirely in SwiftUI with Swift Charts, ships telemetry-free, and is his first app to run natively on all five Apple device families. He also speaks at developer conferences on mobile app security, including iOSDevUK and swiftCon in 2026. [apps.peterkurzok.de](https://apps.peterkurzok.de) collects his [other apps](https://apps.peterkurzok.de/#apps-heading) and his [talks](https://apps.peterkurzok.de/#talks-heading), with slides and recordings. Find him on [GitHub](https://github.com/pkurzok) and [Mastodon](https://kind.social/@filmaniac).
