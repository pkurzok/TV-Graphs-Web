About TV Graphs

TV Graphs is not another TV show tracker. There is no watched-state, no episode check-off and no calendar. It draws the one thing trackers do not: the shape of a series — the slow build, the golden run, and the season everyone warns you about — plotted episode by episode as a chart, visible at a glance instead of buried in a list.

Search the full TMDB catalogue or browse what is popular right now, open any series, and every episode of every season is plotted by its rating. Switch between one chart per season and a single combined view of the whole run with an optional trend line, and select any point for the episode title and its exact rating.

  • Every episode, plotted. Swift Charts draws per-season charts or one combined view of the whole run, with an optional trend line that shows the direction of travel.
  • Search and discover. The full TMDB catalogue with posters, ratings and synopses — in English, German, Polish, Italian, Spanish and French.
  • Favourites that follow you. Star a series and it appears on your other devices through your private iCloud account. Favourites keep working with no network.
  • AI recommendations. Suggestions built from your own favourites by Apple Intelligence on Private Cloud Compute. Only the titles and first-air years of your favourite series are ever sent — nothing else.
  • Native on every device. Not a stretched phone app anywhere: a real sidebar on iPad, a real Settings window on the Mac, focus-driven posters and ten-foot type on Apple TV, and a layout built for Apple Vision Pro.
  • Private by construction. No account, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third-party SDK. The developer runs no server, so there is nowhere for your data to go.
  • Accessible by default. Full VoiceOver labels on every chart point, Dynamic Type throughout, and Reduce Motion respected.

Why free? The charts are drawn from The Movie Database, whose API is free for non-commercial use; charging for an app built on it would need a commercial licence agreement instead — and giving you the whole app is the better side of that bargain. Every feature is there from the moment you install it: no in-app purchases, no subscription, no trial and no advertising, on any device.

About the developer

Peter Kurzok is an independent developer from Germany building native apps for Apple platforms — previous releases include PlayTales, a companion for audiobook fans, and Photo Memo+. TV Graphs is written entirely in SwiftUI with Swift Charts, ships telemetry-free, and is his first app to run natively on all five Apple device families. He also speaks at developer conferences on mobile app security, including iOSDevUK and swiftCon in 2026. apps.peterkurzok.de collects his other apps and his talks, with slides and recordings.
