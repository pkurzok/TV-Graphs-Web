Features

Built with SwiftUI for iPhone, iPad and Mac — one app, native on all three.

Episode rating charts

Swift Charts plots every episode of every season. Switch between one chart per season and a single combined run, and tap any point for the episode title and its rating.

Search and discover

Search the full TMDB catalogue or browse what is popular right now, with posters, ratings and synopses.

Favourites that follow you

Star a series and it appears on your other devices through your private iCloud account. Favourites keep working with no network.

AI recommendations

Suggestions generated from your favourites by Apple Intelligence on Private Cloud Compute. Optional, and it never leaves your favourites' titles behind.

Native on the Mac

Not a stretched phone app. A real sidebar, a real Settings window at Command-comma, and full keyboard navigation.

Accessible by default

Full VoiceOver labels on every chart point, Dynamic Type throughout, and Reduce Motion respected.

A look inside

Charts, search, favourites and settings.

Through your own private iCloud account, using Apple's CloudKit. That storage is yours: I have no access to it and receive no copy. If you do not use iCloud, favourites simply stay on the device.

Recommendations run on Apple's Private Cloud Compute, which needs iOS 27 or macOS 27, supported hardware, and Apple Intelligence switched on. When any of that is missing the tab is hidden rather than shown broken — and the Settings screen explains which piece is missing. Everything else in the app works regardless.
